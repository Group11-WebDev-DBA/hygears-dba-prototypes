Hygears HQ System (DBA): design directions

The internal system for the Hygears HQ: attendance kiosk at the door, dashboard, attendance log, people, items and restocking, borrowing requests, reports and settings. Each direction is built on the same schema (hygears.sql) with a different design skill. Prototypes with sample data; nothing is saved. Brief
